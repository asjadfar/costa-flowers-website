# Costa Flowers — Florist Website, Dehesa de Campoamor

A modern, mobile-first 1-page website for **Costa Flowers**, a florist at
C. comercial Bosque de Lomas, Ctra. San Miguel de Salinas, 2, 03193 Dehesa de
Campoamor, Alicante, Spain.

**Live demo:** https://asjadfar.github.io/costa-flowers-website/ (once published — see below)

## What it shows
- Sticky navigation with mobile menu + floating call button
- Hero with the shop's own pink-lilies bouquet photo, 4.9★ Google rating (62 reviews)
- Same-day delivery bar — Costa Blanca, Alicante, Murcia & Vega Baja; ready-made bouquets from €25
- About — local florist, same-day delivery, in-store shopping, curbside pickup, wheelchair-accessible entrance, cards & NFC payments
- Services (8 cards, each with a real shop photo): Bouquets & Arrangements, Weddings & Bridal Bouquets, Funeral Flowers, Birthdays & Celebrations, Mother's Day & Gifts, Flowers & Plants for Home, Flower Boxes & Baskets, Same-Day Delivery
- Gallery — 12 real Costa Flowers photos from Google Maps, no stock photos
- Reviews — 4.9 stars from 62 Google reviews; quotes from Marion, Sonia and Patrick
- Delivery areas — Costa Blanca, Alicante, Murcia, Vega Baja, Dehesa de Campoamor
- Visit Us — shop hours (Mon 9 AM–6 PM, Tue closed, Wed–Sat 8 AM–6 PM, Sun closed), delivery every day 9 AM–5 PM, pickup Mon–Sat 9 AM–5 PM / Sun 9 AM–12 PM; phones +34 865 77 05 70, +34 632 35 26 19, +34 693 77 33 07; Facebook (THECOSTAFLOWERS), Instagram @thecostaflowers; embedded Google Map
- SEO meta tags (title, description, Open Graph) + Florist JSON-LD schema

## Photos
All 16 photos in `images/` are Costa Flowers' own photos downloaded from their
Google Maps gallery (shop front, flower stall, bouquets, baskets, shop interior).
No Unsplash / stock photos used. No funeral-wreath photo was visible on the
listing, so the Funeral Flowers card uses a white-lilies photo.

  a third (+34 693 77 33 07) appears on the shop's own Christmas poster — all
  three are listed in the contact section.
